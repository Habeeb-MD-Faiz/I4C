Section 01
Brand Identity Guide

How I4C presents itself, what it stands for, how it sounds, and what it will never be.

01

Name & Wordmark

Care4Poor presents
Ideate4Change
Shorthand: I4C  ·  The numeral is never substituted with the word "for"
"Ideas that challenge. Causes that matter."

The numeral "4" carries the Care4Poor DNA — deliberate, purposeful, immediately recognisable as part of the same family. In all written communication the full name is Ideate4Change. In shorthand contexts, I4C. Never written as "Ideate for Change."

The word Ideate is the brand's verb — it positions I4C not as an event organiser but as an action. To ideate is to generate, challenge assumptions, and build from nothing. Combined with 4Change, it tells the entire story: sharp young minds generating ideas that move the world, while the money those ideas raise moves causes forward.

02

Tagline & Motto

Primary tagline

Causes that matter."

The primary tagline declares what I4C is without explaining it. Ideas = the competitions, challenges, and workshops; causes = the charitable mission that every event funds. It does not exhort or motivate — it states. The secondary tagline is used in school outreach and editorial contexts.

03

Mission Statement

I4C curates high-quality academic and innovation events — competitions, workshops, and ideation challenges — for school and college students who are ambitious, intellectually restless, and ready to act.

Every event is built around a named cause. Every registration fee and sponsor rupee funds it. Every winner is a voice that has earned the stage.

We are not a school fest. We are not a corporate workshop. We are the space where the sharpest young minds come to prove what they're made of — and leave knowing their brilliance changed something real.

04

What We Do — The Two Formats

— Format I
Competitions & Challenges

Ideathons, innovation challenges, mini-Shark Tank pitch competitions, chaos labs, future tech expos. Structured, high-stakes, shareable outcomes. Build I4C's reputation for rigour and attract ambitious students and school/college partnerships.

— Format II
Workshops & Masterclasses

AI literacy, personal finance, entrepreneurship, public speaking, stock market, digital safety, content creation. Expert-led, skill-building, immediately applicable. Stable registration revenue, easier to produce, deeper audience relationship.

Both formats always connect to the month's cause. A personal finance workshop in September might tackle how financial literacy can lift families out of poverty — the cause isn't a footnote, it's the brief. The event IS the fundraiser.

07

Brand Personality

Intellectually rigorous

Our events have a point of view. Judging is meritocratic, formats are thoughtfully designed, the bar is high. We attract students who want to be challenged, not just celebrated.

Quietly prestigious

We don't announce ourselves loudly. We let the quality of our events, speakers, and winners build a reputation that schools and students come to us for.

Genuinely purposeful

The cause is real. The funds go there. We never use "charity" as a badge — we use it as a consequence. Students come for the event; they leave knowing they did something.

Elegantly designed

Every touchpoint — certificate, brief, backdrop, social post — is treated as brand collateral. Nothing is generic. Nothing is hastily assembled.

Student-first

We speak the language of ambitious students — their references, their hunger, their frustration with systems that underestimate them. We are, unambiguously, on their side.

Urgently optimistic

The causes are serious. But I4C's energy is agency, not grief. Come here and leave believing you can actually change something — because you just did.

08

Voice & Tone

Sharp but never cold. Write like someone who has thought hard about this and still gets excited when a student's idea lands. Precision without distance.
Purposeful but never preachy. We believe in causes deeply. We don't lecture. The event funds the cause — that mechanism speaks for itself.
Ambitious but never arrogant. We set the bar high because we believe students will clear it. That's faith, not gatekeeping.
Institutional but never bureaucratic. The brand should feel like a prestigious institution — reliable, consistent, serious — but the writing stays alive and human.

Positioning Statement

Internal use — strategy and partnerships

For academically ambitious school and college students in Indian cities — I4C is the only academic event series that combines the intellectual rigour of a well-designed competition or workshop with the moral clarity of a single, named cause per event. Unlike school fests that prioritise participation over quality, or NGO events that use students as volunteers rather than protagonists, I4C puts the student's mind at the centre — and makes both the event and the cause better for it.

11

Reference Models

I4C adapts proven formats at a focused scale with a charitable layer — rather than inventing from scratch.

Model
Shark Tank India (Junior format)
Takeaway: Pitch competitions are viscerally engaging, create natural video content, and the format is already understood by Indian students. Replicate: Mini-Shark Tank as a recurring I4C format — student founders pitch social ventures to a judge panel. Winner gets a small grant from cause fund.
Model
MIT / TEDx Student Chapters
Takeaway: Prestige through curation, not size. A 50-person MIT demo day is more prestigious than a 500-person conference. Replicate: Quality of thought over headcount. Invest in certificate design, expert judges, and publishing notable student ideas from Day 1.
Model
Enactus India / YouthCo (social enterprise competitions)
Takeaway: Cause-integrated competition. Teams solve real social problems as the competition brief. Replicate: Every I4C ideathon brief is tied to that month's cause. Winners' solutions shared with beneficiary NGO.
Model
MUN Circuits (self-replicating community)
Takeaway: Past participants become next year's organisers and referrals. The community fuels growth. Replicate: Build alumni + volunteer pool from Event 01. Past participants get early access, volunteer roles, and brand association.
